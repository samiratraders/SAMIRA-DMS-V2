# SAMIRA DMS V2

## Master Business Rules

Status: Draft v1.0

This document is the foundation of the entire SAMIRA DMS.

No database, backend, mobile or web module will be developed unless the business rules are finalized.

## Core Principles

1. Single Source of Truth
2. Transaction Driven System
3. Approval Before Final Posting
4. No Direct Stock Editing
5. No Permanent Delete
6. Complete Audit Trail
7. Ledger Based Reporting
